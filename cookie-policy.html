Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Framework: Consent-First Architecture Updated: [FILL THIS: October 21, 2026]

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and browser local storage are small data text snippets stored on your device by your web browser. Under India's DPDP Act 2023, non-essential cookies and client-side identifiers that track users require prior affirmative consent.

2. Our Zero-Tracking Without Consent Commitment

We do not inject any non-essential, analytics, or third-party telemetry trackers until you click "Accept All" or enable them explicitly in our Cookie Preference settings.

3. Detailed Inventory of Cookies and Storage Keys

Key / Name Type Category Lifespan Purpose
theme localStorage Strictly Necessary Persistent Stores your preferred visual theme (dark mode or light mode).
dpdp_consent_preferences localStorage Strictly Necessary Persistent Stores your explicit cookie and data processing consent choices.
visited Cookie Functional / Analytics 24 Hours Deduplicates daily portfolio visitor counters. Only set upon consent.
visitorCount localStorage Analytics Persistent Maintains cached count of portfolio page views.
cf_clearance / CDN Cookie Security (Essential) Session Used by CDN / DDoS protection layers to prevent bot abuse.
