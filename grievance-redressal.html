3. Grievance Procedure & Resolution Timelines

  1. Step 1: Submission of Grievance:
    You may submit your grievance by email to vikash.jmbox@gmail.com with the subject line "DPDP Privacy Grievance - [Your Name]", or by submitting a ticket through our My Data Portal.
  2. Step 2: Acknowledgment (Within 48 Hours):
    Every grievance will receive a unique ticket reference number and formal written acknowledgment within 48 hours of receipt.
  3. Step 3: Investigation & Redressal (Statutory Max: 90 Days):
    We commit to investigating and resolving all privacy grievances within our internal target of 30 calendar days, and in no event later than the statutory maximum of 90 days prescribed under the DPDP Act 2023.

4. Escalation to the Data Protection Board of India

If your grievance is not resolved within the statutory period, or if you are dissatisfied with the resolution provided by our Grievance Officer, you have the statutory right under Section 13(3) of the DPDP Act 2023 to escalate and lodge a formal complaint with the:

Data Protection Board of India (DPB)
Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India
