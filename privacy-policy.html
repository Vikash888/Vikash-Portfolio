6. Your Statutory Rights as a Data Principal (Sections 11–14)

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you possess the following enforceable rights:

  1. Right to Access Information (Section 11): Right to obtain a summary of personal data being processed and identities of Data Processors with whom it was shared.
  2. Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): Right to rectify inaccurate or misleading data and demand the erasure of personal data that is no longer required.
  3. Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): Right to readily available grievance redressal through our appointed Grievance Officer.
  4. Right to Nominate (Section 14): Right to nominate an individual who, in the event of death or incapacity, shall exercise your rights.
  5. Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your consent at any time as easily as it was given. Processing carried out prior to withdrawal remains lawful.

To exercise any of these rights seamlessly, visit our interactive My Data / Privacy Rights Portal.
